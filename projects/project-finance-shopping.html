Shipped Experience

Now live on CarMax.com and in stores, this finance-based shopping experience helps customers explore vehicles within their approved financing terms and monthly budgets.

The Challenge

Many CarMax customers apply for financing before shopping for vehicles, but the existing experience didn't effectively connect financing approval to vehicle selection. This disconnect resulted in customers being uncertain which vehicles fit their budget, leading to abandoned purchases.

Key Finding: 50% of the customers who were approved for financing ultimately did not purchase from CarMax. This gap revealed a disconnect between how customers understood their financing terms and how those terms related to the vehicles available to them—making it harder for them to shop confidently.

Goals

Redesign the post-financing-approval experience to seamlessly connect customers with vehicles that fit their budget and approved terms.

Target Impact: Improve app-to-sales conversion by making it effortless for customers to find and purchase vehicles within their financing approval.

The Solution

I redesigned the experience to focus on financing and monthly payments, making car shopping more transparent and personalized. Integrated into CarMax’s omni-channel strategy, it connects the online and in-store experiences into one continuous journey.

  • Payment-first browsing: Show monthly payment prominently on every vehicle card, making budget comparison effortless
  • Approval-aware filtering: Guide customers to vehicles within their approved financing range with clear visual indicators
  • Unified car card component: Created consistent vehicle presentation across web, mobile, and in-store touchpoints
  • Clear post-approval flow: Streamlined journey from financing approval to vehicle selection with contextual guidance

Results

The finance-based shopping redesign delivered meaningful business and customer impact:

  • 8% increase in app-to-sales conversion for customers with approved financing, demonstrating the value of payment-first browsing
  • 29% reduction in post-approval drop-off by seamlessly connecting financing approval to vehicle selection with clear budget indicators
  • 83% of customers found budget-appropriate vehicles using the new filtering and payment prominence features
  • Increased customer confidence — Qualitative research showed customers felt "more in control" and "less stressed" about staying within budget. Sales associates reported customers arriving to stores better prepared, with one noting: "Customers know exactly what they can afford now. They're not wasting time on cars outside their range." Post-purchase surveys indicated ~70% of customers using the finance-first flow felt more confident in their purchase decision compared to the previous experience
  • Reusable design system — The unified car card component was adopted across web, mobile, and in-store touchpoints, becoming a foundational pattern in CarMax's design system

Learnings

Key takeaways from this project:

  • Payment-first mindset aligns with customer behavior — Most customers think in monthly payments, not total price
  • Cross-platform consistency drives trust — Unified car card component created seamless experience across web, mobile, and in-store
  • Testing with real customers reveals edge cases — In-store observation uncovered financing scenarios not captured in data
  • Design systems accelerate future work — Reusable components enabled faster iteration and consistent experiences
